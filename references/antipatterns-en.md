# Default anti-patterns: English

Starting list for the personal skill. Remove any item the author actually uses (check the corpus and ask).

## Anti-patterns

"delve", "delve into", "notably", "importantly", "crucially", "interestingly", "underscore", "underscores",
"pivotal", "crucial role", "intricate", "multifaceted", "tapestry", "landscape of", "in the realm of",
"a growing body of evidence", "shed light on", "sheds light on", "pave the way", "paves the way",
"it is worth noting that", "it is important to note", "in today's", "navigate the complexities",
"furthermore", "in summary", "in conclusion", "overall,", "plays a key role", "plays a crucial role",
"serves as a testament", "a testament to", "not just", "game-changer", "robust framework", "seamless",
"leverage", "harness", "unlock", "embark", "elevate", "foster"

## Constructions (not matched by the script; check by reading)

- "not only ... but also" and "not X, but Y" reversals used for emphasis
- triads of adjectives or nouns ("complex, dynamic and fascinating")
- em dash (—) as an aside (the script counts it)
- short punchy sentence after a long one, for effect ("This is important.")
- paragraph closing that restates the paragraph
- every sentence opening with a connective
- rhetorical questions in expository prose
